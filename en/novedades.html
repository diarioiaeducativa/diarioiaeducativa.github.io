10 NovemberAnthropic, models

Anthropic launches Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2, with the former open to everyone

The company announces two versions of the same model: Fable 5.2, with safeguards and available to all, and Mythos 5.2, only for verified organisations.

7 NovemberUsage policy

Anthropic bans sustained, gratuitous abuse of its models in its usage policy

The update, published on 8 October 2026, takes effect on 12 November and will be enforced only in extreme cases.

3 NovemberHigher education

MIT report calls for rethinking teaching and assessment in the age of AI

The committee set up by MIT to study the use of AI in teaching, learning and research training recommends AI-aware educational processes and a review of assessments and policies.

31 OctoberTeachers' digital competence

European Commission updates DigCompEdu to include AI and digital wellbeing

The European framework for teachers' digital competence is under revision, with a final version expected in spring 2027 and consultations with teachers.

29 OctoberNew Google model

Google unveils Gemini 4 Carbon, for now only for cybersecurity specialists

Google DeepMind has announced its new frontier model, which for now is used only by a small group of cyber-defence professionals. There is no date for the general public.
