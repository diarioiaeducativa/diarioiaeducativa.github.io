Thursday, 19 November 2026

  • Suno launches Visuals to create lyric videos and cover art from its songs

    The feature generates clips of up to 15 seconds and is paid for with credits separate from those for songs.

Wednesday, 18 November 2026

  • Google Flow will retire Veo 3.1 Fast, Veo 3.1 Quality and Omni Flash 1.1 on 5 December

    Flow’s help page recommends moving to Omni Flash 1.2 or Veo 3.1 Lite for future projects.

Tuesday, 17 November 2026

  • EyeJack Creator: bringing an image to life on your phone with augmented reality

    A website lets you attach a video or animation to an image and watch it on your phone by pointing at it. The free version limits views, duration and size.

Monday, 16 November 2026

  • Seedream 5.5 promises to keep an image stable across 100 rounds of editing

    ByteDance presents a version of its image generator designed for retouching the same image again and again without changing what is left untouched, and which generates transparent backgrounds.

Saturday, 14 November 2026

  • ElevenLabs’ Scribe v3 cuts Spanish errors by 35%, according to the company

    ElevenLabs’ new speech-to-text models reduce word errors compared with Scribe v2. The figures are the company’s own.

Friday, 13 November 2026

  • Unreal Engine 5.9 Preview adds MCP so AI agents can create 3D environments

    Epic Games has released a preview version of the engine with AI features, all of them experimental.

Thursday, 12 November 2026

  • Three models draw an animal cell diagram in SVG with no content errors

    A teacher asked three advanced models for a diagram of an animal cell. None made content errors, and they put the reliability down to the vector format.

  • A second video with Fable 5.2 adds an agent that checks the scientific content before generating it

    After the photosynthesis animation, the same teacher has made one on cellular respiration with a reviewing agent, and has translated the first into Catalan.

  • “Polvo eres”, a stop-motion animated short made with generative AI

    A member of the group has shared their first short film, a spoof of cute horror made with generative AI.

Wednesday, 11 November 2026

  • A single prompt is enough for Fable 5.2 to produce a photosynthesis animation for sixth-form students

    With a single prompt, the model produced a square video with a natural voice aimed at sixth-form students. The author considers it good enough for class and speaks of a new way to create materials.

Tuesday, 10 November 2026

  • How “Buzz” was shot: Claude, Blender via MCP, a virtual reality headset and Seedance

    Its director details the process behind the short film the paper covered yesterday, and warns that it is expensive in credits.

Monday, 9 November 2026

  • A short film is shot inside virtual reality and finished with AI

    Raül Torres shares a preview of “Buzz”, a short film whose shots he directs with a virtual reality headset on sets created in Blender.

Saturday, 7 November 2026

  • Google Flow charges credits per second for Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash videos

    Flow’s official help page sets out the cost of generating and editing clips with the model, which can already be chosen in the app, according to comments in the group.

Friday, 6 November 2026

  • Google unveils Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash, which generates and edits videos of up to 15 seconds

    The new version of Google’s video model extends clip length and allows clips to be refined over several turns. The announcement was shared in EduMedia-IAG.

Thursday, 5 November 2026

  • A tool turns a mobile phone and VR headset into a virtual camera for Blender

    Raül Torres describes how, with help from Claude, he built a system that records real camera movements in Blender to then guide an AI video generator.

Tuesday, 3 November 2026

  • A teacher recommends building infographics in HTML and SVG rather than as generated images

    A member of Vibe Coding Educativo argues that, to illustrate concepts, AI should generate SVG shapes and icons in an HTML page, with generated images kept for when they are pedagogically necessary.

  • A website gathers motion-graphics videos with their prompts and skills for agents

    MotionVideo offers AI-made videos accompanied by their prompts, and skills that teach a coding agent how to do motion design.

Sunday, 1 November 2026

  • An AI-made horror web series documents its workflow step by step

    “Never Sleep Again”, seven episodes released between 25 and 31 October, explains on its website how it combines Blender, Claude, a video generator and DaVinci Resolve.
