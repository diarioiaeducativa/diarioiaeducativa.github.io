Republishing a whole story

The newspaper's texts and drawings are licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0: they can be copied to another website, including whole stories, on these conditions:

  • Credit the newspaper and link to the original story. The «For your website» button on each story writes this at the end for you.
  • If you change anything, say so.
  • Publish your version under the same licence.

If a story is corrected or withdrawn from the newspaper, your copy does not change by itself: we would be grateful if you did the same. The link to the original always leads to the up-to-date version.

Join the conversation

Educational AI Daily reports on what is discussed in three open Telegram groups where teachers share what they try with artificial intelligence. The conversations, in Spanish, can be read without joining; to take part, just join.
