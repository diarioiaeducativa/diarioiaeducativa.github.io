13 NovemberCommunity resource

Relleu Obert turns a piece of real map into a 3D model that can be printed for the classroom

A free, open-source project lets you pick a place, generate its relief in the browser and export it for 3D printing.

13 NovemberNew in the repository

A tool helps apply the order of priority in admissions to Valencian conservatoires

José Luis Miralles Bono has created a website with diagrams, an age calculator and Excel templates for applying Order 8/2026 in music and dance schools in the Valencian Community.

13 NovemberCommunity resource

A Catalan-language website tells the story of the spice trade from Marco Polo onwards with maps, games and questions

“El comerç de les espècies” offers nine interactive chapters, designed, according to its author, for secondary school and sixth-form students.

13 NovemberNew in the repository

Reloj EDUmind brings together timers for the classroom, group work and independent study

Luis has created an installable web app with three timer modes, designed for managing time in class from nursery to vocational training.

12 NovemberCoding with Fable

Anthropic recommends telling Fable 5.2 the code is its own if you want it to reorganise it

Anthropic's guide to Fable 5.2 explains that the model behaves differently depending on who is named as the owner of the code, at a cost in tokens.

12 NovemberCommunity resource

Eduvers brings together interactive Physics and Chemistry simulations and is preparing 3D virtual labs

Its author is adding simulations to the website and announces that he will link 3D labs in the coming weeks.

11 NovemberCommunity app

A music-class app asks students to place the notes of a score on the stave

Pentagrama Maestro, created by Iñaki Fernández, plays note by note and offers three levels and unlimited games.

11 NovemberNew in the repository

Tribunator organises panels, rooms and timetables for a conservatoire's entrance exams

José Luis Miralles Bono has created, with the help of Claude, a tool that plans who sits on each panel, in which room and at what time they examine, and checks for clashes before anything is published.

10 NovemberAgents at home

A group thread uses coding agents to control home automation

A thread in the group collects uses of agents such as Claude Code or Antigravity to run the television, music and the Home Assistant configuration, with control of privacy as a condition.

10 NovemberCommunity apps

Two Catalan-language apps by David Cordones: a letters and numbers game and a reception classroom

Xifres i Lletres trains vocabulary and mental arithmetic by age; Aula d'Acollida Digital offers initial language support.

10 NovemberCommunity website

A website promotes Belsetán, an endangered Aragonese variety, with games and classroom worksheets

“El aprendiz del pastor” brings together history, texts, games and teaching materials on the language of the Bielsa valley.

9 NovemberNew in the repository

A planner lets schools build the school-year calendar from the official Valencian Community one or from scratch

Calendari, created by José Luis Miralles Bono with the help of Claude, lets a school build its academic calendar in the browser, starting from the official Valencian one or from scratch.

8 NovemberCommunity app

Holmio turns the periodic table into a daily detective game

Each day brings a new case with 118 suspects, the chemical elements, and six accusations to find the culprit; according to its author, no registration is required.

8 NovemberNew in the repository

A calculator splits the working week of peripatetic teachers across several schools in the Valencian Community

Itinerancias, created by José Luis Miralles Bono, calculates how the non-teaching hours are divided among the schools of a teacher who is shared between them, according to the teaching hours they give in each one. It opens in the browser, in Spanish and Valencian, and also offers an Excel spreadsheet with the same calculation.

8 NovemberCommunity app

Incluxat brings the guidance from educational psychology reports to the conservatoire

An AI assistant adapts to music education guidance designed for mainstream schools, with its supporting documentation published so it can be reviewed.

7 NovemberCommunity app

A teacher replaces the school siren with a musical bell managed from a spreadsheet

David Cordones says that, with the help of generative AI, he built a school bell on a Raspberry Pi that staff change from Google Sheets.

7 NovemberThe repository's week

An auction of phrases, a guide to responsible vibe coding, chemistry, music and illustrated books: new in the repository

Since 27 October, the Vibe Coding Educativo resource repository has added sixteen resources: ten have already had their own article in the paper and six are presented here, two by Juan José de Haro, one by Felip Sarroca, one by FiQuiPedia and two by Samuel Soriano.

7 NovemberCommunity app

EDUmind MiClase, an open-source teacher’s notebook, keeps data on the device and syncs without its own server

Its author explained in the group how it syncs two devices and which features he says he has added to assess under the LOMLOE.

6 NovemberShared resource

MechSimulator brings together free mechanical engineering simulators that run in the browser

A group member highlighted this website of 2D and 3D simulations as an example of what one person can achieve.

6 NovemberNew in the repository

Fil i compte lets teachers prepare printable maths problem sheets in Catalan and Spanish, with an answer key

A bank of 252 problems lets primary and lower secondary teachers pick problems, put them in order and print the worksheet or save it as a PDF.

5 NovemberScience in 3D

An open project offers 54 interactive 3D science lessons for upper secondary school, in Chinese

Tongjiang Lab brings together physics, chemistry, biology and geography lessons in HTML files that work offline; in the group it was pointed to as an example of what will be possible with AI.

5 NovemberNew in the repository

A tool randomly forms the home and expert groups for the Jigsaw technique

“Rompecabezas – Técnica Jigsaw” takes a list of students and subtopics and splits them into balanced groups. It opens in the browser and is available in five languages.

5 NovemberNew in the repository

A block-based programming editor, built on TurboWarp and compatible with Scratch, for learning to code

EduTicTac Blocs is a visual programming environment compatible with Scratch projects and with classroom templates; according to its website, it has no advertising or trackers.

4 NovemberCommunity resource

A gamified activity takes the gravitational field in Year 13 Physics to nine orbital missions

“Misiones orbitales”, by El profe de la bata, asks students to create an astronaut and complete nine missions using interactive simulators. The practice version runs in the browser, with no accounts.

4 NovemberNew in the repository

A Catalan-language lab lets students practise inorganic formulation with questions, hints and challenges

“Laboratori de formulació”, created by Felip Sarroca i Gil, offers inorganic nomenclature exercises for Secondary and Upper Secondary students, with three working modes and a progress counter.

4 NovemberNew in the repository

FoQo generates organic formulation tests and gives the name of a drawn structure

FiQuiPedia’s app lets users practise organic chemistry nomenclature with configurable tests and a tool that goes from formula to name.

4 NovemberCommunity resource

A Geography teacher uses Gemini to build a counter of births and deaths around the world

“Pulso Demográfico Mundial en Tiempo Real”, by Ricardo Chao Prieto, simulates in real time the births and deaths in each country, based on the UN’s population projections for 2026.

3 NovemberSecurity and agents

Giving a command-line agent full access means protecting students’ data

An experienced teacher explained what an AI agent with full control of a computer can do and what information should be kept out of its reach.

2 NovemberSchool app

A Catalan school shares an app for student self-assessment and teacher feedback

“La teva veu” (“Your voice”), created by Felip Sarroca for Escola Ramon Pont, generates PDF reports for families and teaching staff.

1 NovemberPhysics with AI

An interactive lab guides Year 13 students through gravitational potential

The app “Mapa de potencial gravitatorio”, shared by teacher Antonio González, includes a nine-step teaching guide and calculates the work done between two points.

1 NovemberTyping practice

An interactive activity teaches how to type the symbols on the Spanish keyboard

The exercise, shared by Toni Magraner, trains symbols, accents and shortcuts with a test mode and a training mode.
