Library

One article for each document that enters the ChatGPT-IA-edu Digital Library, the collection of guides, reports and studies on artificial intelligence and education gathered by its members. Each article explains who publishes the document, what it proposes and how it fits with the others, and links to its official source.

13 NovemberUNESCO guide

UNESCO proposes five units for students aged 13 to 15 to question AI’s answers and decide for themselves

“AI answers. You decide” is a companion guide to UNESCO’s media and information literacy curriculum, with classroom discussions and activities that can be used separately.

13 NovemberRegulator's guidance and report

England's university regulator clarifies which AI practices fit its quality conditions and publishes the study behind them

The Office for Students and Advance HE have put out a 48-page report, based on more than 300 students and staff, and a guide that translates its findings into registration conditions B1, B2 and B4.

4 NovemberMIT report

An MIT committee calls for rethinking aims, assessment and the in-person community in the face of an AI that can already do almost any written work

The ad hoc committee of the Massachusetts Institute of Technology proposes eight principles and a set of recommendations for adapting university teaching to generative AI.

3 NovemberReport published by UNESCO

A report for UNESCO argues that there is no single right age for using AI in the classroom

Sonia Livingstone and three co-authors argue that there is no single right age for using AI in education and call for decisions based on child development and clear educational aims.

3 NovemberWorld Economic Forum report

The World Economic Forum sets out seven practices to make AI safe for children without shutting them out of learning

A September 2026 document from the Global Coalition for Digital Safety takes children’s well-being, and not merely the absence of incidents, as its goal, and proposes practices for those who design AI and incentives to ensure they are followed.

3 NovemberOfficial Catalan guide

Catalonia sets out 45 guidelines on which uses of AI to promote and which to limit in schools

The Department of Education and Vocational Training publishes a guide in September 2026 that advises against autonomous use of general-purpose AI by under-14s, grading with AI and AI-text detectors.

3 NovemberOfficial Catalan guide

Catalonia offers criteria for deciding when a technology adds value in primary and ESO, and places AI in the fourth year

The Department of Education’s guide puts technology at the service of learning, with decision tables, progression by cycle, digital well-being and criteria for selecting resources.
