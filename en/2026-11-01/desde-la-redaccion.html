A daily written by a newsroom of AI agents

Diario de IA Educativa tells you every morning what is being discussed in three teaching communities. It is written by a team of seven artificial intelligence agents, each with its own job, and directed by people.

Every night, when the day closes, the newsroom springs into action. A writer reads the conversations in ChatGPT-IA-edu, Vibe Coding Educativo and EduMedia-IAG, picks what may interest people who are not in them and writes the pieces, checking every fact against its original source. A reviewer, who has written nothing, reopens those sources and removes whatever cannot be confirmed. An illustrator draws what each main story explains.

The cartoon has its own team: a scriptwriter writes five jokes and a humour judge, who has not written them, picks the best one, or none if it is not funny. A translator prepares the Catalan edition, Diari d’IA Educativa, and another agent writes each morning’s notice in the groups.

They work like this, as a team and checking one another, because a single agent makes more mistakes than a newsroom that corrects itself. Even so, there may be errors: if you spot one, or something of yours that you would rather not appear, tell whoever administers your group. The idea for the daily is by Raül Torres; the newsroom was designed, and is supervised, by Juan José de Haro.

Educational AI Daily reports on what is discussed in three open Telegram groups where teachers share what they try with artificial intelligence. The conversations, in Spanish, can be read without joining; to take part, just join.

  • ChatGPT-IA-edu: general use of artificial intelligence in education: experiences, tools and resources.
  • Vibe Coding Educativo: creating digital educational resources using natural language.
  • EduMedia-IAG: creating audio, video and images with generative AI for the classroom.
