A year ago

Every day, something the groups were discussing on the same day a year earlier, told from today: what has changed, what no longer exists and what time has settled.

13 November 2025AI literacy

The European AI literacy framework expected in December arrived in June, with the PISA 2029 assessment in the background

In November 2025, anyone looking for ways to teach critical thinking with artificial intelligence was pointed to a framework still in draft, with the final version announced for December. It arrived six months later.

12 November 2025Language models

GPT-5.1 arrived in ChatGPT to amazement at its speed and did not last four months there

In November 2025, people were struck by how quickly GPT-5.1, OpenAI’s “more conversational” model, arrived. By 11 March 2026 it had already disappeared from ChatGPT.

11 November 2025Maths with AI

Gemini’s formulas reached Google Docs as code; a year on, Docs still does not understand LaTeX

In November 2025, Gemini was already writing and editing formulas in LaTeX, but when they were taken into Google Docs they arrived as code. A year on, Google’s word processor still cannot read LaTeX and the remedy is the same: an add-on or a separate editor.

10 November 2025Open models

The open model that was “checkmating” the giants: a year on, Kimi is almost three times bigger and still behind

In November 2025, people were sharing that a Chinese open-weights model, Kimi K2 Thinking, beat GPT-5 on some tests. Today Kimi K3, from the same family, is almost three times bigger, but its maker admits it does not match the best closed models.

9 November 2025Apps with memory

A word cloud with a “backend” in a single prompt: today Claude’s new artifacts save data without being published

In November 2025, a word cloud built with Claude stored everyone’s votes from a single prompt. Today the new artifacts save data without needing to be published.

8 November 2025AI video

From the character who warped within eight seconds to models that keep them the same from scene to scene

In November 2025, animating a character with Veo 3 meant cutting the clip before it stopped looking like itself. Today Google promises to keep it consistent from scene to scene.

7 November 2025From NotebookLM to Gemini Notebook

NotebookLM presentations, once impossible to tweak, can now be revised slide by slide

A year ago, anyone wanting an editable presentation had to leave NotebookLM. Today the tool, which no longer even goes by that name, makes slides, amends them on request and exports them to PowerPoint.
