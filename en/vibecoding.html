Vibe coding

Apps that teachers create by describing them to an artificial intelligence, without needing to know how to code, and what the groups learn about making them: instructions for generating resources, coding agents and solutions to common problems. Each app is presented with its author's name, and those that join the Vibe Coding Educativo Resource repository have their article here.

11 NovemberCommunity app

A music-class app asks students to place the notes of a score on the stave

Pentagrama Maestro, created by Iñaki Fernández, plays note by note and offers three levels and unlimited games.

11 NovemberNew in the repository

Tribunator organises panels, rooms and timetables for a conservatoire's entrance exams

José Luis Miralles Bono has created, with the help of Claude, a tool that plans who sits on each panel, in which room and at what time they examine, and checks for clashes before anything is published.

10 NovemberAgents at home

A group thread uses coding agents to control home automation

A thread in the group collects uses of agents such as Claude Code or Antigravity to run the television, music and the Home Assistant configuration, with control of privacy as a condition.

10 NovemberCommunity apps

Two Catalan-language apps by David Cordones: a letters and numbers game and a reception classroom

Xifres i Lletres trains vocabulary and mental arithmetic by age; Aula d'Acollida Digital offers initial language support.

10 NovemberCommunity website

A website promotes Belsetán, an endangered Aragonese variety, with games and classroom worksheets

“El aprendiz del pastor” brings together history, texts, games and teaching materials on the language of the Bielsa valley.

9 NovemberNew in the repository

A planner lets schools build the school-year calendar from the official Valencian Community one or from scratch

Calendari, created by José Luis Miralles Bono with the help of Claude, lets a school build its academic calendar in the browser, starting from the official Valencian one or from scratch.

8 NovemberCommunity app

Holmio turns the periodic table into a daily detective game

Each day brings a new case with 118 suspects, the chemical elements, and six accusations to find the culprit; according to its author, no registration is required.

8 NovemberNew in the repository

A calculator splits the working week of peripatetic teachers across several schools in the Valencian Community

Itinerancias, created by José Luis Miralles Bono, calculates how the non-teaching hours are divided among the schools of a teacher who is shared between them, according to the teaching hours they give in each one. It opens in the browser, in Spanish and Valencian, and also offers an Excel spreadsheet with the same calculation.

8 NovemberCommunity app

Incluxat brings the guidance from educational psychology reports to the conservatoire

An AI assistant adapts to music education guidance designed for mainstream schools, with its supporting documentation published so it can be reviewed.

7 NovemberCommunity app

A teacher replaces the school siren with a musical bell managed from a spreadsheet

David Cordones says that, with the help of generative AI, he built a school bell on a Raspberry Pi that staff change from Google Sheets.

7 NovemberThe repository's week

An auction of phrases, a guide to responsible vibe coding, chemistry, music and illustrated books: new in the repository

Since 27 October, the Vibe Coding Educativo resource repository has added sixteen resources: ten have already had their own article in the paper and six are presented here, two by Juan José de Haro, one by Felip Sarroca, one by FiQuiPedia and two by Samuel Soriano.

7 NovemberCommunity app

EDUmind MiClase, an open-source teacher’s notebook, keeps data on the device and syncs without its own server

Its author explained in the group how it syncs two devices and which features he says he has added to assess under the LOMLOE.
